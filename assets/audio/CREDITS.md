# Combat audio credits

All sources are CC0 / public domain. Processed and layered for this project
in `tools/bake_combat_sfx.py`.

- **Free Firearm Sound Library** (OpenGameArt) — `gunshot.wav`, `gunshot_sniper.wav`
- **Gunshots** by various / OpenGameArt CC0 — 22 Pistol, 22 Magnum, Black Powder takes used in `gun_pistol_*`, `gun_revolver_*`, `gun_smg_*`, `gun_rifle_*`, `gun_shotgun_*`
- **Kenney Sci-fi Sounds** (CC0) — force-field + low-frequency explosion layers in `repulsor_*` only (no retro laser “pew”)
